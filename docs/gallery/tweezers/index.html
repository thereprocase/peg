Tips down. A longer, steadier throat.

The new throat follows the broad upper body over 58 mm. Low side rails locate its edges, with closer guidance over 19 mm. The lower broad face carries the tool; the upper arm stays free. There is no opposed jaw to squeeze the arms closed, and no floor beneath the points.
