Yes, it is a generator.

The supplied Pegstr OpenSCAD modules generate these bodies. Set profile, columns and rows; the flush back and custom peg spacing follow the array. The ZIP includes the library, pegs and all eight presets.

Opening dimensions and pitch are checked against the archived originals. The supplied generator has slightly different outer rounding and gussets; P09 is 0.33 mm shallower. The original models remain available above. These are form-review models, with the chosen print face shown; they have not been sliced or physically tested.
