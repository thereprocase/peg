MB01 F v1 - magnetic shaft bar, two-seat form/function test.
99.6 mm wide, four 25.4 mm grid cells; 50.8 mm tool centers.
One nominal 3 mm shaft seat (R1.65), one 8 mm shaft seat (R4.2).
Shallow half-rounds open forward; 30 mm vertical bearing length.
Flat unrecessed top canted 5 degrees toward the board supports root/collar.
Two 10x10x3 mm block magnets, magnetized through the 3 mm thickness.
Magnet-to-shaft nominal minimum gap 1.02 mm; pull strength is untested.
Body rereads canonical tighter peg fit; two hooks plus lower locators.
Matching 0.8 mm hook-tip flats share the top print plane.

Print TWO separate plugs first, using their broad-face print file.
Print holder flat top down in the supplied print pose. Local peg supports
are expected. Pause BEFORE closing the magnet chimneys at geometric
print height 28 mm. Drop in magnets, then the 14.8 mm-long plastic plugs.
The magnet top is 15 mm below that pause plane; the plug is recessed 0.2 mm.
Resume to close the 2 mm caps. These are geometric instructions, NOT an
approved slicer pause or G-code. Check the actual sliced closure layer.

Valid one-body holder, STEP round trips, nominal straight-forward and
lift/forward removal, grasp envelopes and pause loading were checked.
No slicing, physical fit, magnetic force, heat or hotend-attraction test.
Planning tools are generic diameter/hand envelopes, not measured models.
Installed and print poses are alternatives; do not print both holders.
Yellow in cutaway = magnet. Blue = separately printed closure plug.
