Magnetic screwdriver rack C v1

Four standard drivers · seven grid cells · 175.8 mm wide · current canonical tighter pegs.

Model review, not a ready-to-run print. Magnet size is provisional: four 10 × 10 × 3 mm block magnets, magnetized through the 3 mm thickness. Magnetic pull, plug grip and thermal suitability remain untested. A and B are preserved.

The intended motion

The flat seat carries the handle. A 120° V saddle locates the shaft below it; the magnet supplies gentle rearward preload. Lift approximately 6 mm to clear the shallow seat, then pull forward. Reverse that path to return. This geometric path is clear for the nominal 6.35 mm shaft / 30 mm flat-shouldered handle envelopes; actual shoulder shapes and magnetic release force still need a trial.

Pause and load

  1. Print four plugs first, lying on their supplied broad face. Check plug fit before committing to the rack. The 0.125 mm-per-side upper ribs are intentional interference, not a proven holding force.
  2. Print the rack upright on its broad base. All four rectangular loading pockets finish at the same height.
  3. Pause before the first closing layer, with the head parked away. The CAD opening is at 37.6 mm above the rack's print base. Only for uniform 0.20 mm layers including the first layer, this corresponds to after layer 188 / before layer 189. Verify that against the actual sliced toolpath; no pause G-code has been generated.
  4. Drop each magnet edge-first down its 10.4 × 3.4 mm loading pocket. Its square face faces the shaft. Push a plug down onto it. The plug's top is designed to sit 0.2 mm below the pause plane, with no material protruding into the next layer.
  5. The magnet top lies 15.8 mm below the closing plane. The long plug occupies 15.6 mm above it. Resume only with the inserts seated and retained; distance alone is not a guarantee against attraction to the toolhead. A 2.4 mm printed cap closes the chimney beneath the handle seat.

The V-apex skin is 0.8 mm; nominal shaft-to-magnet distance including cavity clearance and V geometry is approximately 1.49 mm. Magnet grade has not been selected. Test the chosen magnet and actual shafts before relying on this retention.
