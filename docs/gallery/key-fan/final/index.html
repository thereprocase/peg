The socket concept

  • Full straight engagement: max(40 mm, 8× shaft size), so a 10 mm key has 80 mm of guide.
  • Separate chamfer: 3 mm along the shaft, opening 1.5 mm outward per flat. It adds to the guide length.
  • Square stop and 1 mm vent: an axial floor vent connects to an outward passage below the socket, preserving stop shoulders.
  • Handle clocking: flat-to-flat with the T bar; six straight flats throughout the hex guide.

3 mm and 5 mm are physically selected fits. Applying C clearance to other sizes and print angles is provisional. Small keys may still rotate in ideal geometry; a long guide reduces rocking but does not guarantee clocking. Torx sockets use nominal blade envelopes and need their own fit trial.

One rack per set: 33034 (T9–T40), 13189 (2–10 mm), and 13190 (3/32–3/8 inch). The two staggered tiers bring their depth close to the original fan.

What is checked

Each delivered model has native solid and mesh checks, full straight guide clearance, a continuous 2 mm socket-wall check, clear vent routes, retained floor shoulders, unchanged board-side connector geometry and nominal stored-tool clearance. The saved layouts also have nominal sampled withdrawal screens.

These revised models have not had a complete rack print, support-release test, physical installation or load test. The old v2 FEA and installation results describe different geometry and do not qualify these models. The old whole-host installation collision remains relevant to the mounting approach; a new path is still needed.

Print meshes are bare, unscaled inputs in the left-end orientation. Use your P1S/ASA profile, apply shrink compensation once, and review supports and the compensated bed/brim footprint. The brace STL is an aligned modifier for 100% infill, not a separate printable part. No G-code or printer profiles are included.
