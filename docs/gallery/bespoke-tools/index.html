The gray shapes are clearance references: dimensioned hex-key models, a conservative tape-case envelope and a coil envelope. The tape pocket allows a case plus clip up to 92 × 48 mm; exact brand fit needs a physical check. Hex-key references and their sources are listed under Tool reference CAD.

Slice settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, two Arachne walls, 20% infill, organic supports and a 5 mm outer brim. Supports are confined behind the mounting face. The tape dock has about 0.001 g of bridge extrusion at the pointed window apex. Reslice for your printer and filament.

The stiffness calculation assumes solid isotropic ASA. Printed infill, layer bonds and board compliance affect the physical result. Check support release, spring action and tool retention on a first print.
