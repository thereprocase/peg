Four racks, zero support extrusion

Each B rests on its 55° diagonal underside. Actual Orca slices generate zero supports with automatic supports enabled at 50°, internal support allowed, bridge exemption disabled, and no raft or brim. All four also pass the sampled layer-start check. The original separate tip floors are unchanged and are outside this support-free claim.

Flat-seat experiment, not universal anti-tip retention.

The backward-rock check now uses the correct direction: handle toward the board, shaft toward you. Nominal flat handles first hit the holder at about 28°; undersized handles move farther, and one 28 mm handle in the 40 mm seat remained clear through 60° with 0.5 mm lift. The flat floor is a more direct bearing surface, but the low rim does not positively arrest rocking early. Rounded or mismatched shoulders remain unqualified.

What is checked

Valid single-solid STEP bodies, closed STL meshes, unchanged canonical pegs, chosen bed plane at Z=0, all 19 nominal grasp and removal paths, deliberate bearing, forward-slide interception, backward-rock probes, full-surface angle screening and actual Orca extrusion. Tiny fixed-peg facets total less than 0.002 mm² below the 49.5° screen; Orca produces no support for them.

No physical print yet. The inherited PETG review profile reports a bed-temperature warning, so no G-code or ready-to-print profile is offered. Use your calibrated filament settings for a trial. CAD/renders are downloadable; provisional Craftsman handle sizes remain assumptions.
