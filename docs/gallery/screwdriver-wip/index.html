Screwdriver stations.

The current experiment is the lettered magnetic shaft sampler. Try your screwdrivers one at a time, then report the letters that feel right. Those choices will set the seat sizes for the next everyday rack.

Current test print · MS01 v2

Find your letter

Two A–P strips: round shafts from 2–8 mm in half-millimetre steps, plus three quarter-inch hex clearances. Sized for your 20 × 10 × 3 mm magnets, eight per strip.

14 mm preprinted plugs leave 1 mm nominal clearance. Print plate 1 first, then either strip. The P1S / 0.4 / PLA job includes the magnet-loading pauses.
