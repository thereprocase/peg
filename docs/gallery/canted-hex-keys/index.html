Canted metric hex-key rack

Nine full-depth sockets lean 30° outward from vertical. The long arms slide completely inside; the bends and short arms remain exposed, pointing toward the desk.

HX02 v1 · prototype. Physical print, fit, handling and support release remain pending. The key references use the supplied straight lengths, with assumed bends and short arms.

201.2 mm wide · 23 mm between socket centres · two mounting columns.

Pull along the socket axis, upward and outward. The 10 mm key needs approximately 220 mm of axial travel to clear, moving 110 mm outward and 191 mm upward. Leave that path clear of nearby tools.

Print on the broad right cheek. Each internal socket has a pointed roof for this orientation, and the lower peg hoops flex in the print layers.
