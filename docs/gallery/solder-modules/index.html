B6 · seven separate mounts · tighter peg fit throughout

A solder station built around its print faces.

Tweezer mount updated to v7 after physical rocking feedback. Loaded assembly views below retain the earlier mounting backs; use the v7 individual download and rear view. Earlier slice evidence applies only to the other six modules.

The same tools and independent access, split into seven pieces on the one-inch grid. Overall mounting span: 302.8 mm. Barrel and tray modules stand on their bases; the narrower tool and reel modules print on their right cheeks.

All seven rear interfaces match rear-tongue-3p80-interference0p05: nominal 0.05 mm interference at 3.80 mm board thickness. This relies on plastic/board compliance. All mounted gallery downloads now use this shared fit.

Print review: all seven slice in Orca on the P1S 0.4 mm profile without mesh repairs. Supports remain under pegs, short guide lips and some curved features; inspect the amber toolpaths below. The barrel rack has the most support, about 15.6 g. The inherited PETG profile raises a bed-temperature warning, so machine G-code is not included. Use your verified material settings. Physical surface finish and fit remain to be tried.

The barrel grips remain exposed between short bearings. Each tool has a checked lift-and-depart path with its neighbors present. Tweezers lift along their slope and leave through the open left side. The liner lifts 13 mm, then slides forward below the stored nozzles. These checks use the existing planning tools, not new measurements.
