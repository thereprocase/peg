The lineupScrewdriver WIPMS01 v3 sampler

The letters became holders.

MH01-MH04 v1 · form review. The MS01 trial picked four seats: E, I, M and O. Each now has its own magnetic bar with the same seat, the same 0.8 mm seat skin, the same flat 5 degree bearing top and the same tight pegs (fit e586ab4ff6) as the sampler you mounted.

CAD only so far. Not sliced, not printed. Magnets are sealed: the print pauses at 38 mm, each 20 × 10 × 3 mm magnet and its plug go in, and the last 2 mm caps them permanently. The pause layer must be verified in the slicer before anyone prints these.

They tile on the grid

Width is 25.4 mm × cells − 2 mm, with pegs in the two outermost cell centres. Seat pitch is cells × 25.4 ÷ seats with the row centred, so identical bars hung side by side keep the same seat spacing across their 2 mm seam. Eight handle-clear seats of I or M need 355-406 mm, more than one P1S print, so each eight-seat row is a pair of four-seat bars.

What has been checked

One valid solid per holder; STEP round trips; both peg flats on the bed plane; seated tool clear of the holder; bearing contact under a 0.1 mm drop; lift-then-forward withdrawal with every neighbour seated; 0.99-1.04 mm magnet-to-shaft gap, matching sealed v2; straight magnet and plug loading path at the pause; print bounds under 250 mm.

Not established: slicing, supports, pause layer, physical fit, load, and magnetic feel at this spacing. You judged the letters in the open v3 channel, where the magnet gap could range 0.82-1.62 mm; the sealed pocket holds it near 1.02 mm.
