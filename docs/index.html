A SIMPLE PLACE TO JOIN

Line up the shoulder. Fuse the nub.

The orange cylindrical nub extends 2 mm into the holder. Its diameter is 20% larger than the reference shaft: 6.72 mm on the upper hook, 7.62 mm on the hoop and locator. The flat shoulder around it marks the holder’s rear plane.

  1. Import in millimeters. Use STEP or FCStd. Individual connectors share an X = 0, Z = 0.12 mm hole axis; pairs already have 25.4 mm vertical spacing.
  2. Align the flat shoulder to the holder’s rear face. The reference plane is Y = 0.15 mm. The nub points toward +Y and must sit inside the holder body.
  3. Boolean union. Use Fusion Combine → Join, Onshape Boolean → Union, or FreeCAD Part → Fuse. Give the nub real shared volume with the body.

Choose the hoop orientation to suit the completed holder’s print layers. Review upper-hook overhangs, supports and installation clearance on the whole assembly. The connector files are in design coordinates.

The board-side fit geometry is unchanged. Native round-trip and sample union checks passed; physical fit and load capacity still depend on your board and print. The original nominal anchor’s motion certificates cover that earlier geometry.
