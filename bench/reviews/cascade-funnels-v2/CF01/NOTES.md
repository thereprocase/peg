# CF01 v2 · plain-mouth cascading funnels

The front-visible beveled rim bands have been replaced with plain continuous funnel mouths. This revision removes the outer rim bevel and the flared inner rim treatment. The exterior front faces run directly to their mouth edges.

The three funnels still touch at 25 mm centres. The right seat is highest; each seat steps down 25.4 mm toward the left. Backplate width remains 99.6 mm. Nominal funnel mouth 22 × 76 mm, throat 11 × 48 mm, and lower exit 14 × 48 mm remain unchanged. The shared receiver retains the original PF02 #9 / PF07 #5 geometry. The lower tubes and rear webs start on the common upright print bed face.

Source: bench/source/cascade_funnels_v2/build.py. Native STEP, FreeCAD and print-oriented STL are packaged with exact source dependencies. CAD validity, unchanged rear interface, sampled tool withdrawal, adjoining-shell overlap, print geometry, nominal installation clearance, solid-ASA stiffness and local ASA toolpaths are checked for this version. Generic closed-tool envelopes represent clearance assumptions, not measured models of the owner's tools. Physical print, fit, hand access and support release remain pending. Through-bores remain open and long tips may extend below the cassette.

Stiffness uses 4 mm nominal second-order tetrahedra, isotropic solid ASA E=1800 MPa and fixed peg nodes. Infill, layer bonds, mesh convergence and actual board compliance remain unqualified. The matching STEP/spec hashes and solver results accompany the model.
