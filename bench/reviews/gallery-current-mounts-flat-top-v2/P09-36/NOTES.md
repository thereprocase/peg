# P09-36 — genuinely flat top, upside-down print, v2

**Selected replacement for the interim cheek-print remount.** The owner requested an actually flat broad top so the array can print upside down. Nine columns × four depth rows remain: 36 nominal 10 mm round seats, approximately 10.4 mm in the supplied Pegstr body.

The source top varied from about Z = 5.02 to 5.12 mm. It is planed at original Z = 5.00 mm, removing **274.4614 mm³** within a maximum **0.1200 mm** top band. The remaining body is translated **+0.60 mm in Z**, without X/Y scaling or smoothing. The resulting **Z = 5.60 mm top is truly planar**. The integral 5.4 mm receiver ends on that same plane. This is authorized top planing, not a claim that all original forward material remains unchanged. Exact checks retain the entire post-planing body with zero missing volume and add no material forward of the mounting wall. Sections at installed Z = -2.4, -4.4 and -9.4 mm each contain all 36 clear openings. Remaining seats retain their original section/depth geometry except the stated thin top removal.

**Print the supplied print.stl upside down, rotation X = 180°**, not the prior cheek pose and not the 175° magnetic-holder compensation. Bare bounds: **122.50 × 71.29 × 37.60 mm**. Actual planar bed contact is **4,306.30 mm²**, of which **3,250.60 mm² is forward body beyond the mounting wall**. The forward body begins at the bed plane. This is broad body contact, not merely a receiver strip.

Full PF02 #9 hooks are retained. Their crown is **0.1550 mm below the new installed top plane**, so they do not float the body off the bed. There are **no crown pads or towers**. Continuous integral upper roots and the 5.60 mm receiver lip clear the board-plane screen through **1,491 nominal reference-route poses** (maximum point movement 0.05 mm, mesh deflection 0.005 mm). The exact rear interface matches the shared recipe with zero symmetric-difference volume. This sampled front-body check does not establish a continuous proof or physical installation of the deliberately interfering peg fit.

Mounting columns remain X = ±50.8 mm. PF02 #9 uses 0.49 mm nominal clamp interference at 3.94 mm board and 0.50 mm upper ribs. The lower row is the corrected PF07 #5 hoop with 0.8 mm arms and 2.95 mm root clip, using the **upright/horizontal hoop construction** for this inverted print. Its X flex stays exactly within XY print layers; the cheek-only 0.10 mm correction is not applied. Upper/lower hole spacing stays 25.4 mm.

Installed and print STEP read back as one valid solid. Both STL meshes are watertight, consistently wound single material shells. The bare body fits the recorded P1S 256 × 256 × 250 mm profile with its front-left 18 × 28 mm exclusion avoided. **This is bare CAD/mesh, not a sliced or support-qualified job.** Accessible local peg support is allowed and still needs fresh toolpath/removal review. The planar top solves body bed contact; it does not establish support-free pegs, adhesion, physical fit, retention or a load rating.
