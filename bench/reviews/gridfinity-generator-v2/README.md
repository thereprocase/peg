# Gridfinity generator rail and web revision

Owner requested removal of rear rail extensions and full side-web contact with the front rail. The rear rail is now exactly the frame width, 42 × columns + 4 mm. Complete 10 mm mounting posts stay inside that width; where needed their columns move inward on the unchanged 25.4 mm board grid. PF02/PF07 geometry and the 5.4 mm root material remain unchanged.

The web lower edge reaches the front beam bottom and its upper edge joins within the beam. The web no longer terminates in a point short of the front rail. Seats, clearances, tilt limits and frame-down export remain unchanged.

CHECKS.json records eight native rebuilds: 1×1, 2×1, 2×2, 3×1, 4×1, tight/thick, +5° and −30°. Each passed solid validity, STEP roundtrip, exact board-side preservation, continuous roots, reference-foot checks and saved STL topology. Negative clearance intentionally permits a proud bin.
