## MS01 retaining combs v1 — flat-print add-on

User replaced loose spacers with a connected eight-finger strip held by tape,
then explicitly required FLAT printing. Two combs match A-H and I-P channels;
the existing v3 holders are unchanged and need no reprint. All fingers share a
broad flat face. Thickness varies into the offset channels (A-H 1.25-3 mm;
I-P 2-3 mm); no staggered floating fingers and no upright print pose.

Fingers 16.8 mm with 0.6 mm tapered leads; nominal 0.2 mm magnet end play.
Rails 150.4 mm wide, 4 mm deep. Insert the labeled face toward the shaft slots,
flat back toward the board. Tape rail to body; peel and withdraw to recover.
CAD full slide envelopes and tool withdrawal pass, reference holders compare
identically to v3. Native flat two-comb P1S/0.4/PLA plate: 9.47 g, 30m23s,
no supports/brim/pauses. Settings reslice, bed bounds and startup pass. Physical
fit, rail flex and tape retention untested. No printer job sent.
